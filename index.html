ABOUT OSCN

What we do

OSCN is a bottom-up network of researchers across Nijmegen who are passionate about making Open Science the norm in scholarly practice — connecting people and sharing knowledge. Our goals are to:

Reach & engage

Create opportunities to learn, connect and exchange knowledge about Open Science.

Inspire & enable

Support scholars in exploring and adopting Open Science practices.

Empower & support

Help shape Open Science policies, infrastructure and services.
