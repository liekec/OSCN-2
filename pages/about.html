ABOUT OSCN

Open Science Community Nijmegen

A bottom-up network of researchers, students and professionals committed to making research more transparent, reproducible and collaborative. Guided by openness, fairness, diversity and inclusivity, we aim to foster a more open research culture by connecting people and sharing knowledge.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND OSCN

Organising team

OSCN is coordinated by a multidisciplinary team of researchers, students and professional staff who are committed to advancing Open Science within and beyond Nijmegen. Together, we organise events, facilitate knowledge sharing, and support a more transparent and collaborative research culture.

AE

Anita Eerland

Behavioural Science Institute (BSI), Radboud University

Associate professor of Communication Science and Open Science Officer within the BSI. Anita joined Radboud University and OSCN in 2021 after founding the first Dutch Open Science Community at Utrecht University together with Loek Brinkman in 2018. She is a board member of OSC-NL and formerly chaired INOSC for two years, and co-developed the Open Science Community Incubator Program, a free 14-week programme helping people worldwide set up their own local OSC.

MC

Maximiliano Cenci

Radboudumc University Medical Center

Assistant professor at the Department of Dentistry at Radboudumc, Nijmegen. Max is a clinical researcher focused on decision-making in dentistry and the promotion of research integrity in the field, and works to foster transparency and better research practices through Open Science principles.

LC

Lieke Corbeek

Radboud University

Lieke joined the OSCN team in September 2025. She studied Medical Biology at Radboud University with a specialisation in Science, Management and Innovation, and is currently pursuing a Pre-Master's in Communication Science. At OSCN she supports social media, the website, newsletters, and small member events.

TM

Tim Middeldorp

Radboud University

Tim joined the OSCN organising team in August 2025 as a communication advisor for the Open Science programme. He works for the Faculty of Science as a content specialist, with a background in Cultural and Media Studies and prior experience teaching at a secondary school.

KK

Karin Kastens

Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics

Karin is Head Librarian at the Max Planck Institute for Psycholinguistics, part of the German Max Planck Society. Supporting Open Access and Open Science is central to her work, helping researchers publish open access and exploring new open access and open science formats.
